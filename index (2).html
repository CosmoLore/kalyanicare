KalyaniCare

Protecting Every Pond. Empowering Every Community.

An AI-powered platform that helps villagers, volunteers, NGOs, municipalities and CSR partners restore the traditional ponds (kalyanis) of Anekal Taluk.

Open the live app See how it works

The problem

Aligned with SDG 14 (Life Below Water): the ecological death of traditional village ponds.

Ponds are choking

Kalyanis once supplied drinking and cultural water. Household greywater and sewage overflow now feed toxic weeds and disease-breeding conditions.

No way to measure progress

Local youth groups want to restore ponds but lack a baseline framework to track water quality or coordinate volunteers.

Funds miss the mark

Without continuous tracking, CSR money often goes to short-term cosmetic clean-ups instead of lasting recovery.

Our answer

A transparent, community-led digital registry that tracks each pond's health over time and proves what was restored.

How it works

One connected workflow from the first photo to a verified impact report.

Detect→Verify→Restore→Reward→Sustain

Villager

  • Upload photo or voice report
  • Auto GPS location
  • Track my report

Volunteer

  • View assigned tasks
  • Before and after photos
  • Mark as complete

NGO

  • View all reports
  • Assign volunteers
  • Verify completion

Municipality

  • View ward reports
  • Approve works
  • Budget allocation

Admin

  • Dashboard and analytics
  • Users and roles
  • CSR management
  1. Villager uploads an image and report
  2. Image stored in Firebase Storage
  3. Report saved in Firestore
  4. NGO receives the report and assigns a volunteer
  5. Volunteer uploads BEFORE and AFTER images
  6. NGO verifies completion
  7. Municipality approves
  8. AI generates an impact report and score
  9. Villager earns Aqua Drops
  10. CSR company receives an impact certificate
